The system

The four parts of getting customers.

Marketing usually breaks at one step. Ads that send people to a page where nobody enquires. Enquiries that nobody replies to. We build all four parts, so that doesn't happen.

How it fits together

How the four parts fit together.

Each part feeds the next one. You can start anywhere, but if the part before it is missing, it won't work as well. We'll tell you that before you spend anything.

Traffic

The right people arrive

Facebook and Instagram ads built around 1 clear goal, with different versions tested against each other.

03

Conversion

Visitors become enquiries

The website and landing pages people arrive on, built to load fast, say what you do, and make it easy to get in touch.

What breaks without it

Without it you pay to bring people to your site and then watch them leave.

What you get

The pages, the enquiry forms, and tracking that shows you where each enquiry came from.

04

Follow up

Nothing goes cold

Automatic emails and 1 shared list of every enquiry, so people who are not ready to buy yet still hear from you.

What breaks without it

Without it, the enquiries the first 3 parts paid for go nowhere.

What you get

The emails, the system that sends them, and a login showing you which enquiries have been answered.
