Straight answers

Something else? Ask me directly.

01What does it cost?

Campaigns start from the price shown above, plus GST. That covers the planning, scripts, filming, editing, set-up, running the ads and the report. Your ad spend is separate and you pay it straight to Meta.

02How much should I spend on ads?

At least $35 a day, which is about $1,000 a month. $50 to $70 a day is better, because it gives the ads room to be tested properly. Treat the first month of ad spend as money you can afford to lose while Meta learns who responds. Try the budget calculator.

03What do I need to do?

Be available for a 30 minute call and a half-day shoot, give me access to your Facebook page and ad account, and reply to leads quickly. I handle the rest.

04Who will I be dealing with?

1 point of contact, the whole way through. Planning, filming, editing, running the ads and reporting all come through the same place, so you never have to chase anyone.

05How soon will I see leads?

Usually in the first week the ads are live. The first 2 weeks are mostly testing, so judge the campaign on the whole run, not the first few days.

06What if the ads don't work?

You will know early, because the numbers are in front of you from the first week. I change what isn't working while the campaign runs, and the report tells you plainly what happened. Nobody can promise results, and I won't.

07Is there a lock-in contract?

No. You pay per campaign. If it works and you want another one, we run another one.

08Who is this for?

Small businesses that sell a service and can take on more customers: gyms, clinics, trades, studios and similar. You need a clear offer and someone to answer the phone.
