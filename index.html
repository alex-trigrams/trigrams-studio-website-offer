Straight answers

Something else? Ask us directly.

01What does it cost?

It depends on what you need. A landing page starts from $1,500 + GST. Photos and video are priced on the call, once we know what you need. You always see the price before we start.

02Is there a monthly fee?

Only for your landing page. Hosting is $25 a month, and that covers small changes. Photos and video are paid once.

03How fast is it?

A landing page is usually live in 2 to 3 weeks. For photos and video, we pick a shoot day together on the call.

04What do I need to do?

Have a 30 minute call with us, answer a few questions, and be there on the shoot day. We do the rest.

05Do I need all 3?

You can start with the one you need most and add the others later.

06Will I own it?

Yes. Once it is paid for, your landing page is yours and the domain stays in your name. You get the finished photos and videos to use on your page, your socials and your ads.

07Can you run ads too?

Yes. When your page, photos and video are ready, we can turn them into Facebook and Instagram ads and run them for you. See the ads offer.

08Who is this for?

Busy small businesses in Perth that keep putting marketing off: gyms, clinics, trades, studios, cafes and similar.
