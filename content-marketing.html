Content marketing

Every post pointing at 1 offer.

How a cycle runs

The offer comes first.

Same order every time, which is what makes it repeatable.

Week 1

We settle the offer.

What are we pushing this month, and why would someone act on it now? You have the final say on this, and everything else follows from it.

Week 1

You get the plan.

Every piece for the 30 days, what it says and when it goes out. You approve it before anything gets made.

Week 2

We shoot and I produce.

1 day of filming feeds the month. I cut, write and finish everything, then hand you the assets ready to post.

Weeks 3 and 4

You publish, and we read it.

It goes out on your channels, in your voice. At the end we look at what moved, and that shapes the next offer.

Organic and paid

The same plan, with or without ad spend.

Run it organically and the content builds awareness of the offer on its own. Put budget behind it and the same assets become the ads, because they were made for the same offer. Most businesses start organic and add spend once a cycle proves itself.
