Who's behind it

Video showed me what was missing.

Before any of this I spent 5 years at a managed service provider, looking after the IT for other people's businesses. That's where I learned how a small business actually runs, and how much of it stops working when nobody owns the boring parts.

TRIGRAMS started as a video studio. For 2 years I shot and edited about 20 reels a month for Perth businesses. The videos were good. Most of the time they didn't bring anyone in, because there was nothing behind them. No ads pointing anywhere, a website that never asked for the enquiry, and no follow up when one came in.

So I stopped selling videos on their own and started building the whole thing: attention, traffic, conversion and follow up, in that order. Video sits inside it now. 1 shoot day is still the cheapest way to fill a month of content.

I don't build every part myself. I'm the person you talk to and the person responsible for the result, and I bring in whoever is best at each part, whether that is the ads, the editing or the code. You get one contact and a team behind it.

Most businesses I meet have the same gap. A lead comes in and nobody set up what happens next. Send me a short brief and whatever you already have. I'll tell you which part to fix first.
