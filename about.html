Who's behind it

Video showed me what was missing.

For 2 years TRIGRAMS was a video studio. I shot and edited roughly 20 reels a month for a handful of Perth businesses. It paid. It didn't hold my attention, and I was only using about a fifth of my working hours while still feeling flat out, because the work was repetitive by design.

The question that kept coming up was "why doesn't this business have a system that runs without me." So I built one, then built more of them, faster, with AI doing the heavy lifting.

Video stayed on the menu, because 1 shoot day is still the cheapest way to fill a month of content. It sits inside the system now instead of being the whole job.

Most small businesses I meet have the same gap: a lead comes in and nobody set up what happens next. One email sequence fixes most of it. Send the brief today, and don't wait for a perfect brand deck. I'll work with what you've got.
